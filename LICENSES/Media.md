The font files are unmodified copies; a few were renamed where the upstream file name was cryptic, web-specific or contained spaces (IM FELL, PT Sans, Orbitron). Roboto and Roboto Condensed are the hinted static v2 builds from Google's Roboto repository, which are under the Apache License 2.0; the Ubuntu fonts are under the Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0; every other font is under the SIL OFL 1.1. Static (non-variable) files were chosen on purpose, because WoW renders only the default instance of a variable font. The font dropdown also lists four fonts that ship with the game client (Friz Quadrata, Arial Narrow, Skurri, Morpheus) by their `Fonts\` path; those files are not part of EldoUI and are not redistributed here.

Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans and Poppins match the files the ZerbyUI look uses; their embedded name tables confirm the OFL. ZerbyUI's other two fonts (Gotham Narrow Ultra by Hoefler & Co., and "Naowh", which is ITC Avant Garde Gothic by ParaType) are commercial and are not included. Under the OFL they may be bundled and redistributed with EldoUI, but they may not be sold on their own, and a modified version must not use the original font name. The license text must stay next to the fonts.

## Textures (`Media/Textures/`)

These are all original works created for EldoUI. They were generated procedurally with Python (Pillow and NumPy) and use no third-party artwork (the two logo textures come from the author's own photo, see the Logo section). They are licensed under Apache 2.0, the same license as the rest of EldoUI (see the repository `LICENSE`). `EldoLogo.tga` and `EldoIcon.tga` are made from the troll mascot image in `Media/Logo/` (the author's own photo, see the Logo section) by `tools/build_logo.py`; the wordmark in `EldoLogo.tga` is lettering rendered with Rajdhani Bold. The OFL allows using the font in artwork, and the rendered image is not covered by the OFL.

All files are 32-bit uncompressed TGA with an 8-bit alpha channel and power-of-two sizes.

### Edge-file layout (`EldoShadow`, `EldoGlow`)

The texture follows the standard WoW backdrop edgeFile layout: eight square columns, from left to right:

1. left edge
2. right edge
3. top edge (rotated)
4. bottom edge (rotated)
5. top-left corner
6. top-right corner
7. bottom-left corner
8. bottom-right corner

Opacity is 0 on the outer side and highest on the side facing the frame, so the shadow or glow fades outward. Use it as the `edgeFile` of a backdrop frame that sits outside the panel by `edgeSize`:

```lua
shadow:SetPoint("TOPLEFT", panel, -4, 4)
shadow:SetPoint("BOTTOMRIGHT", panel, 4, -4)
shadow:SetBackdrop({ edgeFile = E.media.shadow, edgeSize = 4 })
shadow:SetBackdropBorderColor(0, 0, 0, 0.8)
```

### Bar textures added in 0.3.2

`EldoSoft.tga`, `EldoMatte.tga`, `EldoSheen.tga` and `EldoCaps.tga` (256x32) are original EldoUI textures generated procedurally with Python (NumPy and Pillow), Apache 2.0 like the rest of EldoUI. Soft is a gentle rounded gradient, Matte a fine-grain matte bar with a darker lower edge, Sheen a flat bar with a diagonal highlight, and Caps a flat bar with a light top and dark bottom line. No third-party artwork was used; they are made to give looks similar to popular community bar styles without copying them.
