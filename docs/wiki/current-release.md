### Bags and loot

#### Fixed

- Right-clicking a potion, food or other usable item in the bag window uses it again.

### Map and minimap

#### Fixed

- Logging in no longer shows a blocked-action error from the minimap, and the minimap now shows the name of the player who pings it.
