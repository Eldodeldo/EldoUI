# Current release

<!-- eldoui:release-docs 0.7.2001 -->

EldoUI **0.7.2001**, 2026-11-11.

[Download this version](https://github.com/Eldodeldo/EldoUI/releases/tag/v0.7.2001) · [All changes](https://github.com/Eldodeldo/EldoUI/blob/v0.7.2001/CHANGELOG.md) · [Updating](updating.md)

These notes describe this version. Migration guides keep the version numbers of the updates they explain.

### Highlights

- Play 47 Flight Games with your mouse or keyboard and resume an unfinished round on your next flight.
- Use the new buttons in a whisper window to invite, look up, add, or ignore the contact, or open your saved conversation.
- Automatic spell rank upgrades now cover every action bar you use and keep lower ranks you place by hand.
- Nameplates show spell, interrupt, and quest text in more alphabets.

### Nameplates

#### Fixed

- The spell being cast, the "Interrupted by" line and the quest line show letters of every alphabet instead of empty boxes, as the names on the plates already did.

### Action bars

#### Improved

- "Upgrade spell ranks automatically" now covers every bar you use: bar 1 with its stance and form pages, every other bar you have turned on, and the gamepad bars. A bar you turn on later gets its new ranks right away. Which bars can narrow it to bars 1 to 3 again.
- A lower rank you drag onto a button yourself stays there; the automatic upgrade leaves that button at its rank.

#### Fixed

- The offer to upgrade spell ranks asks once at login again, not after every loading screen.

### Chat

#### New

- A whisper window has buttons for the contact in its title bar: invite, add friend, who is this, ignore (after you confirm) and your saved conversation with them. Contact buttons on the Instant Messages page turns them off.

### Notifications

#### Fixed

- The ready check window shows the name of the player who started it in every alphabet.

### General

#### New

- Flight Games, off by default: while you fly a flight path a small Games box shows the time left and your last six games, and a click opens 47 games in four groups to play with the keys or the mouse. A game you leave waits for your next flight and comes back paused.
- Flight Games arcade: Snake, Murloc Crossing, Candle Mine, Air Hockey, Rune Stack, Gryphon Raid, Whack-a-Gnoll, Fishing Reflex, Don't Stand in the Fire, Defend the Flight Master (towers beside the road over fifteen waves), Gryphon Glide and Brick Breaker.
- More Flight Games arcade: Kodo Run (jump and duck across the Barrens), Elemental Storm, Loot Catch and Mana Bubbles.
- More Flight Games puzzles: Ley Lines (join the runes and fill the grid), Caravan Jam (free the kodo cart, with the fewest moves shown), Peg Runes on the cross and the triangle, and Word Hunt in English and Danish.
- Flight Games puzzles: Crest Match, Coin Merge, Mines, Rune Memory, Light the Braziers, Sliding Map (with the zone maps), Rune Sudoku, Picture Logic, Pairs, Icon Mahjong and Dwarven Crates, with boards that always have an answer.
- Flight Games cards, dice and board: Solitaire, Dice Duel and Five Dice (the Scandinavian score card), plus Chess on four levels and Four in a Row, Reversi, Checkers, Sea Battle, Five Stones, Stone Pits, Mill and Fence the Pens on three, all against the Horde.
- WoW Trivia in Flight Games asks from more than 1,300 questions about the original game, each with four answers: zones, dungeons, bosses, classes, races, professions and lore. Play 10 or 25 questions, sudden death, or a daily round that is the same for everyone, once a day.
- Flight Games words and quiz also has Daily Word in English and Danish (the same word for everyone on a date, plus free play), Which Spell? (class spell icons) and Where Is It? (find zones and cities on the continent maps).
- Flight Games lay each game on its own table (felt, wood, stone or a night sky) and show the round's time and your games, wins and best streak over the board.
- The Flight Games box opens one of your last games with a click, and "Don't show on flights" turns it off for good.
- Flight Games give Solitaire, Chess, Dwarven Crates, Caravan Jam, Peg Runes and Icon Mahjong buttons for undo, hints and starting over, and Solitaire has larger cards with their own back.
- Flight Games keep your best score in each game and level for the whole account, pause and close when combat starts or you land, and use the game's own icons, maps and creature models with bursts and sparks; open them any time with /eui games.
